# Frieren style

The user's quality target is the magic and fantasy sound of *Frieren: Beyond Journey's End* (葬送のフリーレン, Madhouse, 2023 onward). No Frieren audio could be measured from the container: YouTube and the soundboard sites block it. Everything below comes from staff credits, a fan-wiki list of library sounds, analyses and video essays.

## Evidence

**Subtraction.** Izumo reportedly said the work became "less about what sounds to create and more about how much sound she could subtract". She also said "If Frieren doesn't remember the birds chirping, then we don't hear them". She left out the first footstep of a walking scene. These quotes are second-hand, from a video essay; the Japanese original was not found. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NoAxseBefGU

**Attack magic** (same essay). Spells sound "almost like homing missiles". Offence colliding with defence sounds like "the clashing of two metal objects". Rapid casting sounds like "heavy machine gun". The texture is "snappy and violent ... tearing forward like something launched under immense pressure", in contrast to Harry Potter's "soft, airy" spells.

**Mana.** Analysts describe 「魔力を表す少し金属的な響きの音」 (a slightly metallic ringing sound). Episode 10 marks scene changes with abrupt ambience switches and almost no music. https://note.com/wada_sonnai/n/n1aa087b4b329

**Library sounds heard in the show** (fan wiki list):
- Boom Library "CTDS WHOOSH HIT One Two Trap", a cinematic trailer whoosh-hit
- Sound Ideas "SCI FI, POWER DOWN - DEEP SLICING POWER DOWN OR WHOOSH 02"
- "Anime Glass Break Sound 1"
- Sound Ideas "BELL, HAND - VERY SMALL BRASS BELL: SHORT RING"
- Hollywood Edge forest ambience and birds

Source: https://soundeffects.fandom.com/wiki/Frieren:_Beyond_Journey%27s_End

**Stark vs the dragon** (a sound designer's breakdown). https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UpODFTnlP9s
- The fire charge has almost no sound.
- Swings are close cloth or flag flaps, not synth whooshes.
- A swing is covered by a glass and debris drop.
- The big hit sounds like a metal door or car impact, built up by something like a jet flyby.
- The explosion has several layers: a crunchy mid, a low and wide rumble, a shock-wave whoosh and a very long tail, with a second explosion far back in the mix.
- Distorted debris noise is pulled out when the camera cuts away.
- "Everything ... is being done in small amounts so that the final hit ... sounds so much stronger."

## Brief

| Element | Build it from | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Attack spell (Zoltraak) | a pressurised launch: rocket or missile pass, air tear, a hard crack, a dry short tail | sparkles, laser zaps, soft whooshes |
| Rapid spells | short hard shots 75–95 ms apart, machine-gun rhythm | one long beam |
| Spell on barrier | metal on metal clash, a glass ring, short echoes | energy hums |
| Mana | a very small brass bell short ring, quiet | synth pads |
| Swing or movement | cloth and flag flaps | synth sweeps |
| Big impact | a metal door or car impact, crunchy mid, low wide rumble, shock-wave whoosh, long tail | a single boom |
| Charge-up | almost nothing | long sci-fi risers |
| Around it | silence and sparse ambience | a constant bed |
