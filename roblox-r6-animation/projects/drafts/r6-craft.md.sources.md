## R6 craft: making six parts read human research

Checked 36 findings against their sources with an independent re-read; only supported or partly supported findings (with the checker's correction) are used in [r6-craft.md](r6-craft.md). The sources below were read as text, and the head translation was measured again from the local decodes; no video stream was opened.

| Source | Supports |
| --- | --- |
| [Understanding biological motion through the lens of animate motion processing (Front Psychol 2025, PMC12378459)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12378459/fullTextXML) | Observers recognise human motion from point lights on the major joints alone (Johansson 1973); scrambled displays keep each joint's movement, and walking direction can still be read from them (Troje and Westhoff); inverted displays are rated less animate, possibly through the gravity direction of local motion. |
| [Animal Logic's Rob Coleman Talks About Creating a LEGO World (VIEW Conference)](https://www.viewconference.it/article/87/animal-logics-rob-coleman-talks-about-creating-a-lego-world?amphtml=1) | Animal Logic embraced the limits of hard bricks (no bending, no squash and stretch, no motion blur) for a faux stop-motion style, and changed the style for Batman, who needed a greater range of motion than Emmet. |
| [VFX Voice article on The LEGO Ninjago Movie (Animal Logic)](https://vfxvoice.com/?p=3069) | Matt Everitt: the feeling of a bent elbow is made on the run; each frame hand-crafted; brick blur instead of motion blur; the Jackie Chan look-at-object, look-at-opponent, look-back sequence applied to scenes. |
| [Shigure UI emote, 60 FPS (Roblox DevForum)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/2850808) | A developer notes that R6 animators keep limbs straight instead of adding bendy limbs as Minecraft animators do, and that it works. |
