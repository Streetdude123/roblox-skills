## Acting and performance on six parts research

Checked 38 findings (pending independent check).

| Source | Supports |
| --- | --- |
| [Ollie's Animation Notes, presented by John Lasseter at SIGGRAPH 94 (Frank & Ollie)](https://www.frankanollie.com/AnimationNotesFromOllie.html) | Illustrate thoughts not words or mechanical moves, the mailbox example, lift the body attitude 4 frames before dialogue modulation, cheat staging to simplify, ask whether an added action serves the main idea, and whole-body squash and stretch for attitudes. |
| [Phil on Film, "Don't animate what the character is doing, animate what the character is thinking": an interview with Glen Keane (2011)](https://www.philonfilm.net/2011/01/interview-glen-keane.html?m=1) | Keane passes on Ollie Johnston's rule to animate what the character thinks, and based Rapunzel on his daughter Claire. |
| [Frank Thomas and Ollie Johnston, The Illusion of Life (archive.org text)](https://archive.org/download/disney-animation-the-illusion-of-life/Disney%20Animation-The%20Illusion%20of%20Life_djvu.txt) | One key expression per thought and the speed of its change, Ham Luske on quicker thought, the dialogue lead of three to four frames, accents three frames ahead of the beat, the dwarfs' grief scene, Walt Disney's "aiming", and the follow-through as the punch line. |
| [Shawn Kelly, Animation Tips and Tricks Volume I (Animation Mentor PDF)](https://content.animationmentor.com/pdfs/TipsAndTricks_Volume1.pdf) | Operative words (one per scene, the biggest gesture on it, too many as the main cause of overacting, the sign-language example) and "Do NOT just copy" reference. |
| [Shawn Kelly, Animation Tips and Tricks Volume II (Animation Mentor PDF)](https://content.animationmentor.com/pdfs/TipsAndTricks_Volume2.pdf) | Animate what the character means and wants, act the reference until the words disappear, and body mechanics before acting on a demo reel. |
| [Acting for Animators, notes from Ed Hooks's class (Thinking Animation)](https://thinkinganimation.com/acting-for-animators) | Need over want, intention before results, the body's power centres and their traits, high and low status in posture and gaze, and selective eye contact. |
| [Acting choices: Is it a cliche? (AnimSchool blog, 2017)](https://blog.animschool.edu/2017/05/22/acting-choices-is-it-a-cliche/) | What makes an acting choice a cliché, choosing through personality and subtext (the extrovert's and the introvert's idea), and doing a necessary cliché well. |
| [Reference Analysis: Capture convincing footage (AnimSchool blog, 2025)](https://blog.animschool.edu/2025/07/25/reference-analysis-capture-convincing-footage/) | Scott McWhinnie on Jeff Gabor's reference: framing matched to the shot, real props, one critiqued take at a time, takes with a restricted head, and pushing reference rather than copying it. |
| [Shafir, Tsachor and Welch, Emotion Regulation through Movement (Frontiers in Psychology, 2016)](https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC4707271) | The Laban motor elements that predicted anger, fear, happiness and sadness across 1241 motifs moved by 80 people. |
| [Tracy and Matsumoto, The spontaneous expression of pride and shame (PNAS, 2008)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2575323/) | Judo winners at the 2004 Olympic and Paralympic Games, sighted and blind, raised and spread the arms and expanded the chest; losers slumped the shoulders and narrowed the chest without dropping the head. |
| [Schmitz, Miksch and Einhäuser, Task-irrelevant human and robot head movements bias gaze in humans who follow them through virtual reality (Scientific Reports, 2026)](https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12891715) | Head rotation of an avatar walked behind, even a limbless robot, biased where people looked. |
| [David Gibson: Animating Mei in Overwatch (80.lv)](https://80.lv/articles/david-gibson-animating-mei-in-overwatch) | Heavy characters look strained, Mei's tube limited her moves, the off-balance idle against superhero poses, references for a feeling not for copying, and Mei's soft run. |
| [Eva Sokolova, notes on David Gibson's GDC 2016 Overwatch animation talk (Babel)](https://babel.speldesign.uu.se/?p=113918) | Mei's stance (feet together, slightly off balance, angled away from camera and weapon), references that need not be exact, and the full-extension and passing keys that carry a walk's character. |
| [Jonathan Cooper, Andreas Deja Masterclass Notes (Game Anim, 2013)](https://www.gameanim.com/?p=2897) | Life experience and a hook for the character, selective use of actor reference ("plus" reality), Jafar kept subtle to contrast the Genie, face and hands as the main acting elements, and notes to young animators. |
| [Red post collection: champion animation, reposting RiotLamz's dev blog (Surrender at 20, 2014)](https://www.surrenderat20.net/2014/09/red-post-collection-champion-animation.html) | Yasuo's early ultimate lost in a small space behind effects, three long-seen key poses, and Braum's two-handed shield carry for weight. |
| [Overwatch victory pose cut after fan complains (PC Gamer, 2016)](https://www.pcgamer.com/overwatch-victory-pose-cut-after-fan-complains-that-its-over-sexualized/) | Tracer's victory pose was replaced because it showed none of her personality, while D.va's kept her characterization. |
| [Harold Whitaker and John Halas, Timing for Animation, 3rd edition (archive.org text)](https://archive.org/download/timing-for-animation-by-harold-whitaker-and-john-halas-3rd-edition/Timing%20for%20Animation%20by%20Harold%20Whitaker%20and%20John%20Halas%203rd%20Edition_djvu.txt) | The take (a lag of at least five frames, squash, overshoot, held reaction, longer for a dim character) and the hold lengths for gags, freezes and looks. |
| [Richard Williams, The Animator's Survival Kit (archive.org text)](https://archive.org/download/the-animators-survival-kit/The%20_Animator%27s_Survival_Kit_djvu.txt) | Want and why, one thing at a time, Milt Kahl on protecting the main idea, Frank Thomas on flat but well-moving animation, pantomime first, symmetry for authority, and the traced walk that floats. |
| [Jespone's guide to animations (Roblox DevForum, 2019)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/225752) | A confident idle pushes the torso and aims the head at the objective; the chin touch suggested for a thinking character. |
| [How are my animations? (Roblox DevForum, 2024)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3063720) | A shy idle with one arm gripping the other read as confident from behind, where players usually see characters. |
| [Idle Animation feedback! (Roblox DevForum, 2025)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3484833) | A subtle shopkeeper idle looked like a still image for its first seconds; reviewers asked for a more frequent, larger bob and a head that nods with the breath. |
| [AFK Animation System, need feedback! (Roblox DevForum, 2024)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3178452) | A random idle that started after 3 s without input should wait longer. |
| [Emote specifications (Roblox creator docs)](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/avatar/emotes/specifications.md) | The Animation object is the only component required to upload an emote, with no mesh, texture or other 3D art component. |
