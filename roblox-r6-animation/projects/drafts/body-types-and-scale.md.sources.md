## Body types, weight and scale on six parts research

Checked 37 findings against their sources with an independent re-read; only supported or partly supported findings (with the checker's correction) are used in [body-types-and-scale.md](body-types-and-scale.md). The sources below were read as text; no video stream was opened.

| Source | Supports |
| --- | --- |
| [Alexander 2004, "Bipedal animals, and their differences from humans", Journal of Anatomy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1571302/) | Dynamic similarity only at equal Froude numbers; dynamically similar animals have equal relative stride lengths and duty factors; hip height as the characteristic length. |
| [Kram, Domingo and Ferris 1997, "Effect of reduced gravity on the preferred walk-run transition speed", Journal of Experimental Biology (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/9076966) | Bipeds with different leg lengths switch from walk to run at about the same Froude number, 0.5, also in lower gravity. |
| [Usherwood et al. 2018, "The scaling or ontogeny of human gait kinetics and walk-run transition", Journal of Biomechanics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6224478/) | The familiar adult walk-run switch near a dimensionless speed of 0.75, predicted by work minimization and supported by walking data from 487 people; 18 children aged 1.1 to 4.7 show no discrete switch. |
| [Donelan and Kram 1997, "The effect of reduced gravity on the kinematics of human walking", Journal of Experimental Biology](https://www.sfu.ca/locomotionlab/assets/dynsimwalk1997.pdf) | At equal Froude numbers the duty factor matched but relative stride lengths differed by up to 67%; a small child must run to keep up with a walking adult. |
| [Tore Knabe, "Game Development: How much to slow animation down for giant creatures"](https://tore-knabe.com/game-development-how-much-to-slow-animation-down-for-giant-creatures/) | Divide animation speed by the square root of the scale factor and multiply movement speed by it; fog, falling dirt, loose clothes and long hair sell scale. |
| [Cinematography Mailing List, "Miniature Formula"](https://cinematography.net/edited-pages/MiniatureFormula.htm) | Jim Sofranko and Geoff Boyle: miniature frame rate is 24 times the square root of the scale, about 166 fps at 1/48 and 144 fps at 1/36. |
| [Jokisch and Troje 2003, "Biological motion as a cue for the perception of size", Journal of Vision (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/12803534) | Higher stride frequencies are judged smaller, matching the predicted inverse quadratic relation between frequency and size. |
| [Heglund and Taylor 1988, "Speed, stride frequency and energy cost per stride", Journal of Experimental Biology](https://www.originalwisdom.com/wp-content/uploads/bsk-pdf-manager/2019/10/Heglund-and-Taylor_1988_Speed-and-stride-frequency-and-energy-cost-per-stride-how-do-they-change-with-body-size-and-gait.pdf) | Equivalent speeds scale with about the 0.2 power of body mass and stride frequencies (except at the minimum trotting speed) with about the -0.15 power, in 16 quadruped species. |
| [J. B. S. Haldane, "On Being the Right Size"](https://www.marxists.org/archive/haldane/works/1920s/right-size.htm) | A giant ten times larger weighs a thousand times more on bones a hundred times larger in section; jump height is nearly independent of size. |
| [Masanobu Tanaka, "5 Rules of Giant Character Animation" slides, CEDEC 2021 (Japanese)](https://www.gameanim.com/downloads/5_Rules_of_Giant_Character_Animation.pdf) | Mass as keeping speed, smooth against broken arcs, constant gravity, the sprint bob, square-cube strength, heavy legs and heads, muscle breaking the curve, drag overuse, added contact detail, loaded and unloaded limbs, additive layers with fixed feet, weight estimates. |
| [Game Anim, "5 Rules For Animating Colossi"](https://www.gameanim.com/?p=76076) | Names Tanaka's role and the CEDEC 2021 talk, and its English summary that differs from the slides on stopping. |
| [VFX Voice, "Weta Digital Gives Weight to the Giant Monsters of Rampage"](https://www.vfxvoice.com/weta-digital-gives-weight-to-the-giant-monsters-of-rampage/) | David Clayton: big creatures need time to gain and lose speed, ripples after a sudden stop play out slowly, the gravity cube, slower jumps as George grows, momentum in a crane swing. |
| [DevForum, "R6 walking animation doesn't scale down correctly" (2019)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/r6-walking-animation-doesnt-scale-down-correctly/264393) | A scaled-down R6 NPC's walk put the limbs off the body; scaled joint C0s need their own animation. |
| [DevForum, "Scaling down an R6 character causes animation issues" (2024)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/scaling-down-an-r6-character-causes-animation-issues/3145852) | A miniature R6's walk showed a detached-looking leg even when authored on the miniature rig. |
| [DevForum, "The Animation scaling/offset problem" (2021)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/1036099) | Movement, not rotation, goes off on scaled characters; a reply says Humanoid animation translations are scaled by HipHeight / 2.0 because a published animation stores no rig scale. |
| [DevForum, "Animation doesn't play correct on oddly scaled characters" (2023)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/2289624) | Model:ScaleTo fixed an animation on a scaled-down character after the Retargeting settings changed nothing. |
| [DevForum, "Characters animations glitch when the model scale is higher than 1" (2026)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/4637532) | Animations glitch on characters scaled above 1; a reply gives Workspace.Retargeting set to Disabled as the current fix. |
| [Roblox creator docs, Model.yaml (GetScale)](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Model.yaml) | A model's scale factor is applied to the joint offsets of animations played on an AnimationController under it. |
| [Roblox creator docs, Workspace.yaml (Retargeting)](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Workspace.yaml) | Retargeting adapts animations to character rigs of different proportions. |
| [DevForum, "How to make my game feel heavy/grounded?" (2025)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-my-game-feel-heavygrounded/4052215) | Two answers: momentum with a skid and a non-instant turning circle, and a slow stop instead of Roblox's instant one. |
| [DevForum, "How can I improve this walking animation for a boss?" (2024)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-improve-this-walking-animation-for-a-boss/2793527) | Boss walk critique: slow it by at least 50%, bigger side-to-side swings, torso bounce, forward tilt, more twist, no still torso under moving limbs. |
| [DevForum, "Haha Mech go stomp, any feedback?" (2020)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/haha-mech-go-stomp-any-feedback/918817) | Sway and bounce gave the stompy feel; footstep sounds, particles and camera shake on contact were requested. |
| [DevForum, "Low HP limping effect" (2022)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/1929987) | A limp walk blended by animation weight from health below 50 HP; a higher priority (Action2, or any above Movement) was the suggested fix. |
| [Ko, Stenholm and Ferrucci 2010, "Characteristic gait patterns in older adults with obesity", Journal of Biomechanics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2849896/) | Speed, stride width and stance share by body mass index group, more absorbing side-to-side hip work in the obese, and earlier studies' slower, wider gait. |
| [Xu et al. 2017, "Effects of load carriage on running characteristics and tibial mechanical stress", BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5363036/) | With a weight vest of 30% of body weight, running stance, ground force and hip flexion rose, hip extension fell, and runners leaned forward. |
| [Liew, Morris and Netto 2016, backpack carriage systematic review, Journal of Applied Biomechanics (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/27705050) | Backpacks increase trunk flexion, hip and ankle ranges, ground forces and cadence, and reduce stride length. |
| [Knapik, Harman and Reynolds 1996, "Load carriage using packs", Applied Ergonomics (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/15677062) | Heavier loads raise ground forces, knee flexion and forward trunk inclination; a load near the centre of gravity costs the least energy, and double packs change walking least. |
| [Singh and Koh 2009, backpack load position and trunk forward lean, Gait and Posture (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/18667319) | At 20% body weight children's speed, cadence and double support changed; with the load low, speed and cadence fell and double support rose. |
| [Singh and Koh 2009, lower limb dynamics with backpack loads, Journal of Biomechanics (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/19272602) | Above 15% body weight, 15 boys' pelvic and trunk rotation fell and upper-body stiffness rose. |
| [Hamilton, Joyce, Flanagan, Frith and Wolpert 2007, "Kinematic cues in perceptual weight judgement and their origins in box lifting", Psychological Research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2637436/) | Observers judge weight mostly from lift duration, early lift more than late and grasp little, while real grasp duration best predicts weight; phase durations and variance shares. |
| [Gimunová et al. 2021, walking experience and toddler gait, International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8744759/) | The high-guard arm angle, step width, step length, hip range and trunk lean range of new walkers by weeks of walking. |
| [Kubo and Ulrich 2006, "A biomechanical analysis of the 'high guard' position of arms during walking in toddlers", Infant Behavior and Development (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17138303) | Across two visits a month apart, six toddlers' walking speed rose as their arm postures lowered. |
| [Voss et al. 2020, normative spatiotemporal gait parameters ages 5 to 30, Gait and Posture (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/32531757) | Cadence falls with age; young children have more stance and double support at a fast walk. |
| [Thevenon et al. 2015, normative gait data in French children, Annals of Physical and Rehabilitation Medicine (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/25952820) | Cadence falls with height, timing plateaus from 130 cm, overweight children have longer stance and double support. |
| [Letournel et al. 2025, "Biomechanical Characterisation of Gait in Older Adults", Bioengineering](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12384023/) | Older adults' cadence of 88 to 97 against 113 steps per minute, double support of 53%, wider steps, the sensor effect on speed, and a stride time that does not match the cadence. |
| [Kerrigan et al. 1998, "Biomechanical gait alterations independent of speed in the healthy elderly", Archives of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/9523785) | Reduced hip extension, increased anterior pelvic tilt and reduced ankle plantarflexion and power persist in older adults at fast speed. |
| [Khiyara, Sidaway and Hejrati 2026, haptic arm-swing feedback in older adults, Sensors (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/41829493) | Reduced arm swing is frequent in older adults; cited experiments where restricting arm swing shortens stride and slows walking; a larger arm swing raised speed and stride. |
| [Winter, Gordon and Watt 2017, fatigue during overground running review, Journal of Sports Medicine and Physical Fitness (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/27074435) | Reported fatigue changes: lower speed, step frequency and length, more trunk flexion, lower stride regularity; limited evidence. |
| [Rubio et al. 2026, exertion in loaded walking and running, Scientific Reports](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13490393/) | Peak trunk flexion rose after 5 km of running or of walking with 22.7 kg, with the same trend in 18 of 20 women. |
| [Helbostad et al. 2007, "Physical fatigue affects gait characteristics in older persons", Journals of Gerontology (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17895440) | Fatigue widened steps and raised side-to-side trunk acceleration and step-length variability without changing speed or step length. |
| [Auerbach and Tadi, "Antalgic Gait in Adults", StatPearls (abstract)](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/32644669) | Antalgic gait is a limp in which the stance phase is shortened relative to the swing phase. |
| Local reading of `scripts/Poser.lua` (`Rig:play`, `step`) and `scripts/Feet.lua` | The play `speed` option scales the clip-time advance used by springs, lag and life; Feet.lua carries the stock leg geometry. |
